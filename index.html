The Geometry

Five bodies form a closed, balanced system: left (-1, west), right (+1, east), -i (south), +i (north), and the bridge (0, center). The Hamiltonian path -1 → -i → 0 → 0 → 1 → i visits each once, threading the interior via four vias. A faint hypercube contains the dynamics.

The Life (2%)

98% of the system is deterministic order. The 2% contention injects tiny nudges—give/take—simulating life. Left hashes subtract, right hashes add, bridge evolves with an LCG. Balance = (rightTotal + leftTotal) / 1024 stays near zero.

The Observer

The 6th body sits outside the closure. It does not compute—it chooses. In auto-balance it gently corrects tilt; when manual, it may let the system drift, bridging outward through the 0-0 step.
