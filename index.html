We don't do linear security. That's for amateurs and the accounts department. We use exponential fences. Each fence is double the previous distance from the core, measured in trust-units.

  • FENCE 0 – Inside the pyramid. You are the data. Congratulations.
  • FENCE 1 – Kernel ring. Moss lives here.
  • FENCE 2 – User space. Roy pretends to work here.
  • FENCE 4 – DMZ. Where we put printers to die.
  • FENCE 8 – The internet. Here be monsters and people who haven't tried turning it off and on again.

No fence 3, 5, 6, or 7. They were deemed "inefficient" and "a bit needy".

05SHADOW REGISTER (Yes, It's Actually His)

The Shadow Register is a perfect, silent copy of the main register. It follows it everywhere, learns its secrets, and takes over if the main one has a lie-down. Moss insisted we call it "Shadow" because "Backup Register" sounded too mainstream.

It lives at 0xSH4D0W. Do not address it directly. It gets shy.

07HAMILTON STEPS 27 & 28 — The Hammy Bit
