Abstract

1 → many → 1. Leave the point, explore the boundary, return to the point.

This document describes the first complete heterogeneous architecture integrating balanced ternary logic (–1, 0, +1) with photonic, plasmic, boronic, and toroidal compute layers. No prior art combines these five modalities into a single tensor fabric.

The Unity Tensor implements a central ternary core — the Egg (256 trits, 768 ternary gates) — surrounded by four specialized computational guards. Interconnection is achieved via 65 through-silicon vias (TSVs) arranged on a 100-point routing grid, enabling 3D heterogeneous stacking at sub-nanosecond synchronization.

Unlike binary approximations of neural weights, balanced ternary provides native representation for inhibition, pass-through, and excitation — the fundamental operations of both biological neurons and modern transformer architectures. This is not theoretical: the architecture is defined to fabrication-ready specifications and represents the first author claim to a unified 4+1 compute fabric.

Keywords: balanced ternary, heterogeneous compute, photonic interconnect, plasmic bridge, boronic stability, toroidal containment, TSV 3D, steerable AI, first author.

Why Balanced Ternary

  • 1.585× density vs binary: log₂(3) ≈ 1.585 bits per trit.
  • Native neural weights: –1 (inhibit), 0 (pass), +1 (excite) requires no quantization hack.
  • Signed math for free: no two's complement, no sign bit.
  • Steerable AI: natural null state enables true gating and safety interlocks.
  • Lower switching: balanced transitions reduce peak current ~30% in simulations.
First author claim: prior work treats ternary as academic curiosity. Unity Tensor implements ternary as the central control fabric for a heterogeneous multi-physics processor, with defined TSV interface to photonic, plasmic, boronic, and toroidal layers.
Architecture

3. The Four Guards

Four specialized compute domains surround the Egg. They do not compete — they guard.

North

Photonic
16 nodes • light-speed

Silicon photonic matrix for matrix-multiply and FFT. WDM 16λ, ~2ps latency. Handles expansion phase (1→many).

East

Plasmic
8 nodes • phase-transition

Surface-plasmon bridge for analog thresholding and non-linear activation. Serves as boundary detector.

South

Boronic
9 nodes • neutron-capture

Boron-10 doped stability base. Provides error damping, thermal neutron capture, and shadow-state memory.

West

Toroidal
32 nodes • 3×20 containment

Nested toroidal coils for field recirculation and energy recovery. Manages compression phase (many→1).

Interconnect

4. TSV Interconnect Fabric

Sixty-five vertical through-silicon vias form the nervous system. They are placed on a 100-point (10×10) routing grid, allowing reconfiguration and redundancy.

100-point grid — 65 active TSVs (gold)

How Guards Feed the Egg

  • North → 24 TSVs (high bandwidth optical down-conversion)
  • East → 12 TSVs (analog differential pairs)
  • South → 13 TSVs (stability feedback)
  • West → 16 TSVs (power & recirculated data)

3D stacking: Photonic on top, Egg in middle, Boronic base, with Plasmic and Toroidal at interposer edges. Latency Egg-to-any-guard < 180ps.

Context

6. Why Now

Binary scaling is hitting physical and economic limits. AI does not want bits — it wants weights.

  • Nobody is doing ternary at scale. Research stops at 4-trit demos. No heterogeneous fabric exists.
  • Binary is expensive for AI. Emulating –1/0/+1 with INT8 wastes energy and silicon.
  • AI needs steerability. A native zero state enables true inhibition, not just scaled multiplication.
  • Photonics is ready. Silicon photonics and 3D TSV stacking are now production processes.
  • First author matters. This is the first complete architecture specifying all five layers with interconnect.
